## Boundaries

The inventory covers the ten local retrieval routes above, not every file in the repository or every linked external resource. Prompts, this inventory, the RAG index, the repository register, change/review records and validation code have separate navigation or maintenance roles. They are not additional independent copies of the substantive guidance.

For a historical decision, retrieve its actual recorded revision rather than silently replacing it with current text. For a new task, prefer maintained owning files and preserve their status, authority and limitations. Neither complete source retrieval nor passing structural checks demonstrates production accessibility, audience outcomes, legal compliance or model effectiveness.

External dependencies are recorded in [Public source routing](../Public_Source_Routing.md) and the [repository register](../public-repository-links.json). Public visibility was inspected on the recorded date; specialist and primary sources must still be checked for the actual question. Full external source texts and private materials are not bundled.
